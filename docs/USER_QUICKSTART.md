# SpareLine quick start

SpareLine is our backup chat. Use it when our usual chat, email or meeting tools are down. It runs on our own office server, and your messages are encrypted so that only the people in a chat can read them.

## Join (once)

1. Open **SpareLine**. If it isn't installed, ask IT.
2. Choose **I have an invite** and paste the invite you were sent. You can paste the whole message.
3. SpareLine finds our server automatically. If it doesn't, choose **Enter the address myself** and type the address from your invite.
4. Enter your name, a username and a password, then choose **Join SpareLine**.

SpareLine then waits in the system tray and starts when you sign in to your computer.

## Everyday use

- **New chat** (Ctrl+N): message one person, or start a group chat with people from one of your groups.
- **Enter** sends a message and **Shift+Enter** adds a new line. Press **↑** in an empty box to edit your last message.
- Hover over a message, or tab to it, to **react**, **reply**, **edit** or **delete** it.
- **Ctrl+K** jumps to a chat or searches your messages.
- **Aa** (bottom left) changes the theme, text size, contrast, motion and density. **Ctrl + / −** also changes the text size.
- A line across the top of the window shows the connection. It's solid when connected and dashed while reconnecting. You can keep writing while it reconnects; messages send when the connection is back.

## Trouble connecting?

Go to **Settings → Connection**, choose **Copy report for your admin**, and send it to IT.

## New computer or forgotten password?

Ask IT for a **setup code**. Open SpareLine, choose **I have a setup code**, and choose a new password.
